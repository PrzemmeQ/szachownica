Online chess on a real board — for your club

Students play at home on a wooden board against their clubmates. The phone just reads out the opponent's move and takes theirs — no board on screen, no ads and no chess-site accounts.

Works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. Nothing to install.

Why clubs use it

A real board

Kids practise like at a tournament: pieces in hand, position on the table, not on a screen.

Less screen

A calm app without animations, ads or bait notifications. Parents appreciate it.

Private club

Join with a club code (e.g. HETMA-7Q4MX), optionally also a password — and the coach or an admin approves every new member. You can also add a student by nickname, change the code and remove members.

Coach panel

The coach sees each student's results, watches games live and downloads records for analysis (PGN, Lichess).

Tournament notation

Moves are entered like on a score sheet (Nf3, exd5, O-O) — learning notation along the way.

From 10 minutes

Time to play the move and enter it. Mistakes can be taken back with the opponent's consent.

How to start

  1. The coach creates a club — club name and own nickname. Takes a minute.
  2. Sends the code or link to students (e.g. in the parents' group) or adds them by nickname. A student creates an account and asks to join, the coach accepts; the club is saved in “My clubs”.
  3. Students challenge each other in the club or look for an opponent in the club lobby. Rated games count towards the shared rating.
  4. The coach checks the panel: who plays, with what results, which games are worth discussing in class.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing during testing. If the app catches on, clubs will get clear information about any paid plan well in advance. Playing via Lichess will always stay free.

Do kids need an account or an email?

An account is needed, but no email: just a nickname and password (half a minute). That way the coach knows who asks to join, removing someone from the club is effective, and games count towards the rating. The account works on any device.

What data do you collect?

Only nickname, club membership, games and rating. No addresses, photos, ads or tracking. Data is stored in the European Union. Privacy policy.

How do you know a student plays at a board and not with an engine?

There's no way to check — that's why clubs are private and meant for groups that know each other. The coach sees the games and can discuss them.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone, tablet or computer with an up-to-date browser. The app can be added to the home screen like a normal app. Bluetooth keyboards work too.

Can you play via Lichess?

Yes — anyone with a Lichess account can play there through the same app, on a real board, with their Lichess rating.
